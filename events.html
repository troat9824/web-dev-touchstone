Events & Community

At Book Haven, we believe a bookstore can be more than a place to purchase books. We want to create a vibrant literary community where readers can discover new ideas, connect with one another, and celebrate a shared love of reading through local events and collaborations.

Upcoming Events

Monthly Book Club

Join other local readers for a monthly discussion of a featured book. Readers of all experience levels are welcome.

Local Author Spotlight

Meet local writers, learn about their work, and discover books created by authors in our community.

Children's Story Hour

Families can enjoy a relaxed story hour designed to encourage young readers and build a love of books.

Community Involvement

Book Haven supports literacy and learning by working with local schools, readers, and community organizations. We also welcome suggestions for future events and collaborations.
